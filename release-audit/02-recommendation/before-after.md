# Before / after

Before this audit, recommendation behavior was covered by property and golden tests but had no single release metric surface. The versioned offline harness now runs 16 scenario families and 443 legal candidates without provider calls.

The measured engine produced zero hard violations, full tiny-wardrobe legal recall, exact required/preserved behavior, zero pre-exhaustion session repeats, reproducible seeded output, legal labeled fallback, faithful deterministic/visual traces, and directional Profile + Calibration causality. The largest measured search expanded 2,268 partials and returned 48 candidates.

No scoring weight, beam width, MMR coefficient, constraint, or product semantic was changed: the eval produced no evidence supporting a production algorithm adjustment.
